# Google Play Families policy and child audiences

Established 2026-10-09 from the policy page and web search extracts. The raw pages are not archived under `research/`.

- The Families policy applies when children are among the audiences declared in Play Console (Target Audience and Content). Non-compliance can remove or suspend the app.
- Choose several age groups only if the app is designed for all of them. Child-oriented imagery or wording can change how Google judges the declared audience. Misstating the audience can get the app removed.
- Ads: Families self-certified ad SDKs only, no personalised ads. Not relevant here (no ads).
- Data: disclose any personal data collected from children. Child-only apps must not transmit AAID, IMEI, MAC and similar identifiers, and must not request AD_ID (API 33+) or location. Apps must follow COPPA, GDPR and other laws, and the privacy policy must match behaviour.
- Social features need an adult action before children exchange personal information. The page does not use "parental gate".
- The fetched page did not list the age-group options, so whether an 11+ audience counts as "children" is unconfirmed. From memory, the Play Console groups are 5 & under, 6-8, 9-12, 13-15, 16-17 and 18+. Verify in Play Console.
- UK ICO Children's Code: a child is anyone under 18. It applies to services likely to be accessed by children that process personal data. Sources disagree on whether it is binding. Age-band detail (10-12, 13-15) is from memory, unverified.
- Synth Kit collects no personal data, so most of these duties are met by design. Unverified: whether a no-data app falls under the Code at all.
